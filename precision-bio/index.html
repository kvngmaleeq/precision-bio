Compliance

Built for research use, not retail

Every part of how Precision Bio operates is structured around laboratory and research use — verification comes before catalogue access, and documentation comes before every shipment.

Products supplied by Precision Bio are intended strictly for laboratory research use by qualified, verified research buyers. They are not sold for human or animal consumption, and are not distributed to individual consumers.
01
Research Buyer Verification
Every buyer is confirmed as a research organization, laboratory, or affiliated researcher before catalogue or pricing access is granted.
02
Per-Batch Certificates
Each batch is independently tested, with COA and SDS documentation available to the receiving buyer.
03
Signed End-Use Declaration
Orders are accompanied by a research end-use declaration confirming intended laboratory application.
04
Regulatory Awareness
We monitor applicable regulatory frameworks in the jurisdictions we ship to and adjust distribution accordingly.
