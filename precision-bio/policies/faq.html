Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: August 2026
Who can order from Precision Bio?

Only verified research organizations, laboratories, universities, and affiliated researchers. We do not sell to individual consumers, and every buyer is verified before catalogue or pricing access is granted.

How does buyer verification work?

Submit a research access request with your organization name, research setting, organizational email, and intended research use. We review each request and follow up by email, typically within 1–2 business days.

What is a Certificate of Analysis (COA)?

A COA is an independent lab report confirming a batch's purity and identity, generated via testing methods such as HPLC/MS. Every batch we supply is tested, and COAs are available to verified buyers on request.

Do you provide Safety Data Sheets (SDS)?

Yes. SDS documentation is available alongside COAs for verified buyers, per batch, on request.

How long does shipping take?

Once verified and confirmed, orders are typically prepared within 2–4 business days. Delivery timeframes vary by destination and courier — see our Shipping Policy for details.

Can I return a product?

Unopened, untampered batches may be eligible for return in limited circumstances. See our Refund Policy for full details.

Is pricing listed publicly?

No. Pricing is shared directly with verified research buyers after the access request process, not published on the site.

How do I get in touch?

Email us anytime at support@precisionbios.com, or submit a request through our Research Access form.
