Shipping Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Who we ship to

Precision Bio ships exclusively to verified research organizations, laboratories, universities, and affiliated research buyers. Shipments are only dispatched once a buyer's research affiliation has been confirmed as outlined in our compliance overview.

Processing time

Orders are processed after buyer verification and documentation are complete. Once confirmed, orders are typically prepared for dispatch within 2–4 business days. Processing times may vary depending on batch availability and destination.

Packaging & handling

  • All shipments use discreet, professionally sealed packaging with batch-coded labeling for traceability.
  • Cold-chain handling is used where a compound's stability requires it.
  • Each shipment can be accompanied by its Certificate of Analysis (COA) and Safety Data Sheet (SDS) on request.

Tracking

A tracking number is provided once a shipment leaves our facility. Buyers are responsible for ensuring a suitable, staffed delivery address is available to receive time- or temperature-sensitive shipments.

Delivery timeframes

Delivery timeframes vary by destination and courier and are communicated at the time of order confirmation. Precision Bio is not responsible for courier delays outside of our control, though we will assist in tracking and resolving delivery issues where possible.

International shipping

Where international shipping is available, it is the responsibility of the receiving organization to confirm that importing research compounds complies with the laws and regulations of their country. Precision Bio reserves the right to decline shipment to jurisdictions where compliance cannot be reasonably confirmed.

Questions

For shipping questions on a specific order, contact us at support@precisionbios.com.
