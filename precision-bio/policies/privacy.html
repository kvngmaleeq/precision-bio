Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Precision Bio respects the privacy of the research organizations we work with. This policy explains what information we collect through our research access request process, how it's used, and how it's protected.

Information we collect

When you submit a research access request, we collect:

  • Organization or laboratory name
  • Your name, role, and organizational email address
  • Research setting / affiliation type
  • A description of your intended research use

We do not collect payment information through this website, and we do not knowingly collect information from individuals seeking personal or consumer use.

How we use this information

  • To verify your organization or laboratory affiliation before granting catalogue or pricing access.
  • To communicate with you about your inquiry, quotations, and order documentation.
  • To maintain records required for compliance and end-use declarations.

Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. Access request data may be shared with our form-processing provider (Formspree) solely to deliver your inquiry to us, and may be disclosed if required by law.

Data retention

We retain research access request records for as long as necessary to maintain compliance and order documentation, or until you request deletion.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information at any time by contacting us at support@precisionbios.com.

Cookies

This site does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Basic technical data (such as browser type) may be processed by our hosting provider for security and performance purposes.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be reflected with a revised "last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions, email support@precisionbios.com.
