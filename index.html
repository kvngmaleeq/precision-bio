Compliance

Built for the research supply chain, not retail

Every part of how Precision Bio operates is structured around institutional research use — verification comes before catalogue access, and documentation comes before every shipment.

Products supplied by Precision Bio are intended strictly for laboratory research use by qualified, verified institutions. They are not sold for human or animal consumption, and are not distributed to individual consumers.
01
Institutional Verification
Every account is confirmed against an institutional affiliation — university department, licensed lab, or registered CRO — before catalogue or pricing access is granted.
02
Per-Batch Certificates
Each batch is independently tested, with COA and SDS documentation available to the receiving institution.
03
Signed End-Use Declaration
Orders are accompanied by a research end-use declaration confirming intended laboratory application.
04
Regulatory Awareness
We monitor applicable regulatory frameworks in the jurisdictions we ship to and adjust distribution accordingly.
