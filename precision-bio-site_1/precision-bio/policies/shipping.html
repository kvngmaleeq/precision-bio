Shipping Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Where we are based

Precision Bio is headquartered in Nigeria. Our primary operations, fulfilment, and customer support are all Nigeria-based. All orders are dispatched from our facility in Nigeria.

Who we ship to

Precision Bio ships to individuals and buyers who are purchasing for research purposes. All compounds are supplied strictly for research use only and must not be used for human consumption, diagnosis, or treatment.

Processing time

Orders are processed after buyer verification and documentation are complete. Once confirmed, orders are typically prepared for dispatch within 2–4 business days. Processing times may vary depending on batch availability and destination.

Packaging & handling

  • All shipments use discreet, professionally sealed packaging with batch-coded labeling for traceability.
  • Cold-chain handling is used where a compound's stability requires it.
  • Each shipment can be accompanied by its Certificate of Analysis (COA) and Safety Data Sheet (SDS) on request.

Tracking

A tracking number is provided once a shipment leaves our facility. Buyers are responsible for ensuring a suitable, staffed delivery address is available to receive time- or temperature-sensitive shipments.

Delivery timeframes

Nigeria (domestic): Orders within Nigeria are typically delivered within 2–5 business days after dispatch, depending on the destination state. Lagos and Abuja metro areas may receive faster delivery.

International: International shipments are available to verified institutional buyers outside Nigeria. Delivery typically takes up to 15 business days from the date of dispatch, depending on the destination country and customs processing. Buyers will be notified of any expected delays.

International shipping

Precision Bio ships internationally to verified institutional buyers. International delivery takes up to 15 business days from dispatch. It is the responsibility of the receiving organization to confirm that importing research compounds complies with the laws and regulations of their country. Precision Bio reserves the right to decline shipment to jurisdictions where compliance cannot be reasonably confirmed. Any customs duties, taxes, or import fees are the responsibility of the receiving institution.

Questions

For shipping questions on a specific order, contact us at support@precisionbios.com.
