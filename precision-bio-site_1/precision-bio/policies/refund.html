Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Why our policy is strict

Because Precision Bio supplies laboratory research compounds where integrity and chain-of-custody matter, our refund policy is more conservative than typical retail policies. This protects both the buyer and the reliability of our batch documentation.

Eligibility for refunds

  • Unopened, unused batches only — refunds are only considered for product returned in its original, sealed condition with no signs of tampering.
  • Defective or incorrect shipments — if a batch arrives damaged, defective, or does not match your order, notify us within 48 hours of delivery with photo or video documentation.

Return shipping

The receiving organization is responsible for return shipping costs unless the return is due to our error. We recommend a trackable shipping method, as Precision Bio is not responsible for returns lost or damaged in transit.

Refund process

Refunds are evaluated on a case-by-case basis after we receive and inspect the returned batch. Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of approval.

Non-refundable situations

  • Opened, used, or altered product, for chain-of-custody and safety reasons.
  • Change of mind after an order has been confirmed and dispatched.
  • Delays caused by couriers once a shipment has left our facility.

How to request a refund

Email support@precisionbios.com within 48 hours of delivery with your order reference, a description of the issue, and supporting photos or video. We will confirm next steps once your request is reviewed.
